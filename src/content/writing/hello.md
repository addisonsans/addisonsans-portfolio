This site is one place for all of it — the production work, the
research history, the side projects, and the writing. The previous
version of my site was on Wix, which meant every update was a
manual click-fest in a builder UI. This one is plain markdown:
adding a project is dropping a file into `src/content/projects/`.

A few things I plan to write about here, eventually:

- What I learned shipping LLM/OCR pipelines into government workflows.
- The bits of cognitive psychology research that turned out to be
  useful for thinking about ML systems, and the bits that didn't.
- Self-experiments, and why they're worse than you think (and also
  still useful).
