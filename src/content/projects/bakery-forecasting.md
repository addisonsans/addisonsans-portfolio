A small California bakery delivers fresh product daily, which means
their margin lives or dies on how well they predict the next day's
demand. Order too much, throw it out. Order too little, take an
out-of-stock hit and disappoint a wholesale customer.

I worked with them on the forecasting side: cleaning historical
sales data, building demand and out-of-stock forecasts, and turning
the analysis into **Tableau dashboards** the operations team could
actually use day-to-day — not a notebook they'd have to re-run.

The engagement continued under contract after I rolled off, which
is the outcome I care about most: the system kept running, and the
team kept reaching for the dashboards.
