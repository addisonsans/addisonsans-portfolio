A Jupyter-notebook analysis of the World Happiness Report dataset.
The output is a small set of interactive HTML pages —
GDP-vs-happiness scatterplots, a correlation heatmap, and a few
regional drilldowns.

The interesting part was being honest about what the survey
questions actually measure versus what we'd like them to measure.
