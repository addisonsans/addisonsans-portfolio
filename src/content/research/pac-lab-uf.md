Lab manager at the **Perception, Attention &amp; Consciousness Lab**
at the **University of Florida**, under PI **Brian Odegaard**, from
July 2020 through November 2022.

The role was a mix of research infrastructure and hands-on experimental
work:

- **Built pipelines for new eyetracking paradigms** the lab was bringing
  online.
- **Contributed to experimental design** across concurrent studies.
- **fMRI and neuroimaging data collection.**
- **Set up SQL systems for grad students** so their data lived
  somewhere queryable instead of in scattered CSVs.

I also contributed to **prostate detection research using eyetracking**
at UF — applying the lab's perception methodology to a clinical-imaging
question.

## Publications

I co-authored a peer-reviewed study on metacognitive confidence and
multisensory causal inference in speech perception
(*Multisensory Research*, 2023). A second paper, with **David
Rosenthal** and **Brian Odegaard**, is in progress and pending
submission.
